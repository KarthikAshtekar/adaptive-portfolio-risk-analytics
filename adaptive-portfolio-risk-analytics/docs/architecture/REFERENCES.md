# Key Research Papers and References

## Portfolio Optimization

1. **Markowitz, H. (1952). "Portfolio Selection"**
   - Foundation of Mean-Variance optimization
   - Modern Portfolio Theory

2. **López de Prado, M. (2016). "Building Diversified Portfolios that Outperform"**
   - Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP)
   - Distance-based optimization

3. **Raffinot, T. (2018). "Hierarchical Clustering Based Asset Allocation"**
   - Hierarchical Equal Risk Contribution (HERC)
   - Risk budgeting through clustering

## Covariance Estimation

4. **Ledoit, O., & Wolf, M. (2004). "Honey, I Shrunk the Sample Covariance Matrix"**
   - Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage estimator
   - Optimal shrinkage intensity

5. **Gerber, S., et al. (2022). "The Gerber Statistic"**
   - Robust covariance estimation
   - Rank-sign correlation

## Regime Detection

6. **Hamilton, J. (1989). "A New Approach to the Economic Analysis of Nonstationarity"**
   - Markov-switching models
   - Regime identification

7. **Guidolin, M., & Timmermann, A. (2007). "Asset Allocation under Multivariate Regime Switching"**
   - Multi-asset regime switching
   - Dynamic allocation

## Backtesting & Validation

8. **López de Prado, M. (2018). "Advances in Financial Machine Learning"**
   - Combinatorial Purged Cross-Validation (CPCV)
   - Backtest overfitting
   - Proper time-series validation

9. **Bailey, D., et al. (2015). "The Sharpe Ratio Decoded"**
   - Backtest overfitting
   - Performance attribution

## NLP & Sentiment

10. **Baker, B., et al. (2016). "Measuring Economic Policy Uncertainty"**
    - Policy uncertainty indices
    - Sentiment measurement

11. **Tetlock, P. (2007). "Giving Content to Investor Sentiment"**
    - Media-based sentiment
    - Predictive power of sentiment

## Risk Management

12. **Dowd, K. (2007). "Measuring Market Risk"**
    - VaR and CVaR
    - Risk metrics

13. **Acerbi, C., & Tasche, D. (2002). "On the Coherence of Expected Shortfall"**
    - Coherent risk measures
    - CVaR properties
