What source and scorer were used?

The report uses 32 synthetic headlines from the bundled local CSV and the dependency-light phase4a_lexicon scorer. Positive terms map toward risk-on, negative terms toward risk-off, and near-zero scores toward neutral.

How was look-ahead avoided?

Records are timestamped and assigned to an observed market date. The rolling observed label is shifted by one market session. The decision-source timestamp must precede the decision date.
