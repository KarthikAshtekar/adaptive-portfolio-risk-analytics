# Earnings-Call Corpus Intake Notes

Recommended sectors are Banking, Information Technology, FMCG, Pharma, Energy,
and Infrastructure. Target 2–3 companies per sector and 2–4 quarters per
company.

Use only legally available transcripts. Do not commit paid or copyrighted
content unless redistribution is permitted. Private local transcripts belong
under the ignored `raw/` directory. Always record source URLs, retrieval dates,
sector, company, ticker, and quarter. Run
`python scripts/validate_nlp_corpus_intake.py` before collection.
