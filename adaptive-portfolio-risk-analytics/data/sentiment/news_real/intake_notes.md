# News and Geopolitical Corpus Intake Notes

Recommended themes:

- India inflation
- RBI rate hike
- India liquidity stress
- oil price shock India
- geopolitical risk India
- banking stress India
- currency pressure India
- global recession risk
- supply chain disruption

Avoid pure market reaction articles where the primary content is only that
prices moved. Store only legally permitted text or summaries, preserve source
and provider metadata, and keep private or licensed exports under `raw/`.
Validate publication time, retrieval time, URL, language, and duplicates with
`python scripts/validate_nlp_corpus_intake.py`.
